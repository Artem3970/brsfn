About us

A Milan-based bridge
between Ukraine and Italy.

BORISTENE is an independent Ukrainian cultural association based in Milan. We create projects that make Ukrainian culture legible, contemporary and accessible to audiences beyond Ukraine.

Our work sits between culture, art, history, technology, education and public diplomacy. We believe heritage should not be archived into silence; it should be transformed into experiences, conversations and new relationships.

That is why our projects combine rigorous research with strong visual storytelling, spatial design, digital tools and international collaboration.
