About Boristene
What is Boristene?
Boristene is a Ukrainian cultural association based in Milan, Italy, working to promote Ukrainian culture, history and heritage among Italian audiences through exhibitions, festivals, concerts, conferences and immersive multimedia projects.
Where does the name Boristene come from?
It comes from Borysthenes, the ancient Greek name for the Dnipro River, documented by Herodotus over two thousand years ago. The name reflects our mission: connecting Ukraine's deep cultural roots to Italy and Europe.
When and by whom was Boristene founded?
Boristene was founded in Milan in 2022, at the start of Russia's full-scale invasion of Ukraine, by Svitlana Tereshchenko. It was formally registered under Italian law as Boristene ETS in 2024.
Is Boristene an officially registered nonprofit?
Yes. Boristene is registered as an ETS (Ente del Terzo Settore) in RUNTS, Italy's national register of third-sector organisations, Lombardy office.
What is Boristene's mission?
To engage the Italian public and show the real Ukraine - its history, values and cultural richness - through cultural, educational and artistic projects.
Events & Activities
What kind of events does Boristene organise?
Exhibitions, festivals, concerts, book presentations, film screenings, historical conferences and immersive multimedia projects, including the Ukraine Is Ukraine festival and the Unbreakable Ukraine immersive project.
How do I find out about upcoming events?
Follow our Instagram, Facebook and YouTube channels, or check the News and Events sections of this website. Details are posted as each event is confirmed.
Are events free to attend?
It varies by event. Some are free and open to the public, while others require registration or a ticket. Check each event listing for details.
Do I need to register in advance?
Some events, especially those with limited seating, require advance registration. We recommend registering early for major events.
What language are events held in?
Most events are conducted in Italian, sometimes with Ukrainian or English elements depending on the guests and topic.
Getting Involved
How can I volunteer?
Fill out our volunteer registration form through How to Join Us, telling us your skills and availability. We will match you with a role, from event organising to translation or social media.
Do I need experience to volunteer?
No prior experience is required for most roles. Enthusiasm and reliability matter most; specific skills such as translation or design are welcome.
How can my business become a partner or sponsor?
Use the Partner or Premium Partner registration path and describe your organisation and preferred contribution. Our team will follow up to define the details together.
What's the difference between Partner and Premium Partner?
Partner includes logo placement, event invitations and network access. Premium Partner adds co-founder-level status on flagship projects, VIP access and priority branding at major international events.
Donations & Tax Benefits
How can I donate?
Through the Support Us page, by bank transfer, or by becoming a Member or Partner.
Are donations tax-deductible?
As a RUNTS-registered ETS, individual donations may qualify for a 30% IRPEF deduction, within the applicable legal limits. Contributions must be made by traceable means. Please confirm current thresholds with your commercialista.
What is 5x1000?
5x1000 lets you direct a small share of the income tax you already owe to Boristene at no extra cost. Use Codice Fiscale 97971770157 in the relevant tax-return box and sign.
Unbreakable Ukraine
What is Unbreakable Ukraine?
An international immersive project bringing Ukraine's architectural and artistic heritage to the world through 3D scans, cinematic animation and original sound design.
Where can I see it?
It premiered at the Ukraine Recovery Conference in Rome in 2025, won the top prize at an international immersive art festival in Naples and is scheduled for Ars Electronica in Linz in September 2026.
How can my organisation support the project?
Through a Premium Partner contribution, which can include co-founder status and the right to present the project at your own events by agreement with the project team.
