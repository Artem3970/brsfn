Our Name

Boristene takes its name from Borysthenes - the name ancient Greek geographers, including Herodotus, gave to the Dnipro, the river at the heart of Ukraine. Like the river it is named after, the association sees itself as a channel that carries Ukrainian culture, memory, and voice into Italy and Europe.

Our Story

Boristene was founded in Milan in 2022, at the start of Russia's full-scale invasion, by Svitlana Tereshchenko, out of love for Ukraine and for freedom. In 2024 the association was formally registered under Italian law as Boristene ETS. Since then it has grown into an organisation producing exhibitions, festivals, concerts, conferences and immersive multimedia projects across Italy and on international stages.

Mission

To engage the Italian public, countering distorted information, and to show the real Ukraine - its history, values, and cultural richness - through cultural, educational, and artistic projects.

Vision

By sharing Ukrainian creativity and ideas, we foster collaboration and understanding between peoples and work toward a future in which Ukraine stands alongside its European partners to safeguard shared values.
